Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Cashonary (the "App"), developed by Ricky Herrera. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

* Information Collection: The App operates as an offline-first Point of Sale (POS) system. Business data is primarily stored locally on your Android device using Room Database.

* Cloud Synchronization: To enable cross-device syncing and backups, the App utilizes Firebase Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Storage.

* Authentication: We use Firebase Authentication and Facebook (Meta) Login. When you sign in, we collect your email address and basic profile information solely to authenticate and manage your account.

* Third-Party Services: The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include Google Play Services, Firebase, and Meta Platforms.

* Data Security: We value your trust in providing us with your data and use commercially acceptable means to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
