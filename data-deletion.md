### Data Deletion Instructions for Cashonary

Cashonary takes your privacy seriously. If you have logged in using Facebook and wish to delete your account and all associated data (including cloud backups in Firestore), you can do so by following these steps:

**Option 1: Request Deletion via Email**
1. Send an email to riki4real@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
2. Please send the email from the address associated with your Cashonary account.
3. We will process your request and completely wipe your account data from our Firebase servers within 7 business days.

**Option 2: Remove App Access via Facebook**
You can also remove Cashonary's access to your Facebook account at any time:
1. Go to your Facebook profile settings.
2. Click on **Settings & Privacy** > **Settings**.
3. In the left-hand menu, click on **Apps and Websites**.
4. Find Cashonary in the list and click **Remove**.
